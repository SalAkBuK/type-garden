## Capture invariants

1. Use isolated browser/application state so the user's saved font, material, Look or botanicals cannot change the capture. Read existing source; write only under this new direction's folder.
2. Retain **in bloom** / seed 7 through the whole showcase. Match seed and native writing history across states. For font changes, retain semantic plant identities and clocks while accepting native refit coordinates and actual phrase width.
3. Choose and measure an actual rose → valid glyph collision for the lowercase phrase after final font, size and layout are prepared. Record the selected target, release, impact and native time mapping. Reusing a previous film's target without checking is insufficient.
4. Material states are fresh separate native encounters configured before impact. A contact-sheet comparison must include initial and evolved samples for all four materials, not merely four unrelated stains.
5. Call native scene/plant/rose drawing at the intended capture transform and warm caches so macro images remain sharp. Keep the native layer order, glyph clipping and cast shadows.
6. Distinguish engine time from film time in metadata. Native trajectories, simulation steps and rose states remain authoritative; retiming changes their playback speed, not the underlying mechanics.
7. Capture world/font/botanical endpoint states through supported native controls. Editorial cuts and renderer-derived masks are allowed; they must not be mistaken for automatic product behavior or a smooth font-interpolation feature.
8. The final title is separately authored real planted text, not an unsupported automatic rewrite of the user's phrase. Hold the identity and both attribution lines from 37 to 40 seconds.

The current 48-still capture meets the paired material-state requirement. Its material experiments use the application's supported Fluid-based custom bleeding effect with Amount = 3 on Ink, Blotting Paper, Linen and Wax alike. This quantity change makes the native Wax runoff readable without modifying its pinning threshold or any other material physics. Proof 42 records the detached native runoff drop; its source-rose ID is null, distinguishing runoff from a directly emitted rose drop. The standalone butterfly sequence retains default Legacy crimson bleeding.
